STRANGE DAYS

Book One of the After Dark Chronicles

About the Book

When Georgia Bell's roommate Norma is found brutally murdered—drained of blood through two precise puncture wounds to the neck—the radio host of paranormal talk show After Dark finds herself thrust into a nightmare that blurs the line between the supernatural mysteries she discusses on air and terrifying reality.

Haunted by the memory of Norma's killer, a man named Rick Kaufman who whispered something to Georgia that she can't remember, she teams up with Detective Danny Sullivan to hunt for answers. But the more they uncover, the more impossible the truth becomes: puncture wounds with no sign of teeth marks, victims completely exsanguinated, and a suspect who seems to appear and disappear at will.

When Georgia's investigation leads her into Boston's hidden vampire underworld, she discovers that the monsters from her late-night broadcasts are horrifyingly real—and they've been living among humans for centuries. Caught between the mortal police trying to solve a serial murder case and immortal creatures following their own ancient laws, Georgia must navigate a dangerous game where one wrong move could cost her life.

As Kaufman's body count rises and his obsession with finding his long-lost "Dolly" intensifies, Georgia realizes she may be the key to stopping him.

Urban Fantasy • Thriller • Supernatural Horror
